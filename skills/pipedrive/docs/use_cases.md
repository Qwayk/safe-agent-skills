# What you can do with Pipedrive

Start with a question you can check against a few records.

## Deals and pipelines

- “Show open deals in this pipeline by stage.”
- “Find deals with no recent activity so I can see which ones need follow-up.”
- “Compare the deals we won and lost last month.”
- “Show me the person, company, notes, and files for this deal.”

## People and companies

- “Check whether this contact or company is already in Pipedrive.”
- “Find the people linked to this company and show their open activities.”
- “Show the notes and activity history for this contact before my call.”

## Activities and account settings

- “Show overdue activities for this owner.”
- “Give me a summary for the next salesperson. Include open deals, key contacts, and follow-ups.”
- “List the fields, goals, and permissions we can check in this account.”

Ask the agent to say which records it read, whether there are more results, and what you can check next. Include record IDs or links when they help you follow up.
