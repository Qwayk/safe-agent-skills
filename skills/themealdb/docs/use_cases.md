# What you can ask TheMealDB

- “Find meals with chicken breast. Open one recipe and show me the ingredients I still need.”
- “Find seafood recipes and open the one with the fewest ingredients.”
- “Show me a few Canadian meals and link to the recipes.”
- “Look up meal 52772 and show its ingredients and instructions.”
- “Give me one random dinner idea and tell me what I’ll need to make it.”

Use a recipe's TheMealDB link when you share it. If details are missing, say which ones. [Start with a recipe search](quickstart.md) · [See all commands](command_reference.md)
