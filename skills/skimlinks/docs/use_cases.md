# What you can do with Skimlinks

## Find merchants and offers

Ask "Which merchants sell laptops in the US?" or "What offers does this merchant have?" The answer should include the country and filters you asked for, the merchants or offers returned, and whether there are more pages to check.

## See what earned money

Ask "Which pages or links earned the most commission last month?" or "Which merchants were removed?" For a report, name the dates, how the results are grouped, and which numbers you're comparing. Use the payment status report when you need to check an invoice.

## Find product alternatives

Ask "Does Product Key have alternatives for this product URL?" Skimlinks can look up one product or a list of URLs or IDs. If the account can get a token but the lookup fails, say what Skimlinks returned and which publisher domain the request used.

## Build an affiliate link

Ask "Build a Skimlinks URL for this merchant page and label it newsletter-2026-11." Your agent can give you the original URL and the new link to review. It builds the link on your computer and leaves it unopened. A generated link can earn commission only when Skimlinks can monetize the destination.
