# What you can ask Open Library

- “Find three books about Dune and open the best match.”
- “Look up this ISBN and show me the edition, publisher and year.”
- “Show me the editions of this book. Which one looks like the Spanish translation?”
- “Find Ursula K. Le Guin’s author record and show me five of her works.”
- “Try a small search for books in the foxes subject and tell me what you found.”

Say which record you used. If two books share a title, compare the authors or identifiers before choosing one. [Run a first search](quickstart.md) · [See every command](command_reference.md)
