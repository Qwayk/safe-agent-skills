# Spaceship

Use Spaceship to list your domains, check renewal dates and prices, or review DNS and transfer settings. You can also check SellerHub listings and see where a SafePay deal stands.

Try asking your agent, “Which domains need renewing?” or “Show me the DNS records for example.com.” It starts with a read. If you ask it to renew or transfer a domain or open a SafePay deal, it shows the plan and waits for your approval before sending the request.

## Get started

Ask your agent to install the `spaceship` skill from Qwayk, or run:

```bash
npx skills add Qwayk/safe-agent-skills@spaceship -g -y
```

[Connect your account](docs/onboarding.md), then follow the [quickstart](docs/quickstart.md) for your first check.
