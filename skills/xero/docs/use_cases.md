# What you can ask your agent

Use normal language. The agent can find the matching fixed Xero command, explain the result, and stop for approval before a change.

## Useful first checks

- “List the Xero organisations I connected and let me choose the exact one.”
- “Show the selected organisation and unpaid customer invoices without changing anything.”
- “Compare this month’s profit and loss with last month and explain the biggest changes.”
- “Find draft bills or invoices that need attention.”
- “Check this contact, its invoices, and its recent payments.”

## Accounting and banking

- Review invoices, bills, credit notes, payments, bank transactions, transfers, budgets, journals, tax rates, and reports.
- Find overdue invoices or unusual account activity.
- Prepare a draft invoice, payment, account, or contact change and show the exact plan first.
- Retrieve an attachment to a protected local file or prepare an upload plan.

## Payroll and projects

- Review employees, leave, timesheets, pay runs, payslips, and payroll settings in AU, NZ, or the UK.
- Check project details, tasks, time entries, and project users.
- Prepare a payroll or employment change with the extra approval it deserves.

## Partner-only areas

Bank Feeds, Finance, Payment Services, eInvoicing, payroll, journals, and Projects need a product, role, partner permission, certification, or commercial access. The four App Store billing commands are for legacy XASS transition needs only: Xero deprecated XASS in March 2026 and required existing customers to migrate by 1 July 2026. Practice Manager, Xero HQ, and Xero Tax are documented as access-gated but are not exposed through guessed commands.

## Not a good fit

This tool is not a browser robot, a raw API console, a generic Xero SDK bridge, a webhook server, or a promise of automatic rollback. If the exact operation is not in the fixed coverage ledger, the agent should explain the limit instead of guessing.
