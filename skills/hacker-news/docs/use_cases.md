# What you can ask Hacker News

- “Fetch the first five top stories and tell me what people are talking about. Include the story titles and links.”
- “Check Show HN for developer tools. Open the posts that look relevant and tell me what each tool does.”
- “Show me what jobs are on the jobs list. Open the posts before you tell me what roles they cover.”
- “Open item 8863 and show me its title, author, score and comment count.”
- “Look up this public username and tell me when the account was created and what they’ve submitted.”
- “Show me which items and profiles changed recently. Open the items that match my topic.”

Tell the reader which list you checked and how many items you opened. If you only read Hacker News, say so when the answer depends on the linked article or the full discussion. [Find the exact commands](command_reference.md).
