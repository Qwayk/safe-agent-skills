# Use cases

Start here when you need ideas for what to ask your agent.

## Good first asks

- "Check my connection and show me the first page of domains."
- "Check the domains waiting in the inbound or outbound NameBright account-push queue."
- "Find domains with missing DNS nameserver or contact review issues."
- "Show me a plan for this NameBright ownership move before I approve it."

## Common jobs this tool helps with

- first-pass account checks
- domain availability and readiness checks
- contacts and verification status review
- planned ownership, nameserver, and DNS updates
- safe purchase or renewal planning
- NameBright account-push queue review

## What a good response should include

- a short plain-language summary
- what was read and what is still missing
- the exact plan for write commands
- the required approvals before apply
- any follow-up checks needed after apply
